1. Information the form collects

When you submit a service request, the form saves:

  • Full name: to identify your request.
  • Phone number: to discuss the request with you.
  • Email address: optional, as another way to contact you.
  • City or area and service address: to identify the service location.
  • Selected service: its name and identifier.
  • Preferred date and time window: to understand when you would like the work done.
  • Job details: an optional description of up to 500 characters.
  • Permission to contact: your checkbox selection and the time it was recorded.
  • Request records: a reference number, creation time, request status, and demo-mode indicator.

Do not include payment card details, passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive information in job details.

2. Why it is needed

In this demo, the information is used to save a sample request and display its summary. It is not sent to the business, and submitting it does not arrange an appointment.

For the planned live service, request information will help the business understand the job, check coverage, discuss pricing, arrange a suitable appointment, and manage the enquiry.

The permission-to-contact checkbox concerns your service request. It is not a subscription to marketing messages.

3. Who can see it

Current demo: information is stored in this browser. Business staff cannot view it remotely. People with access to the same browser profile may be able to read it.

Planned live service: customer request details are intended to be accessible only to authorised business staff who need them to manage the request. Customer addresses and contact details will not be displayed publicly.

Before launch, this section must describe the actual hosting and service providers that process request data, and any sharing needed to deliver the service.

4. Storage and retention

Current demo: requests are saved using browser localStorage under the key homeeaseRequests. They remain after closing the page and have no automatic expiry.

Demo records stay until this website's stored data is cleared, the browser removes it, or the data is otherwise deleted. Private browsing or browser settings may prevent storage or remove records when the session ends.

Live retention period: not yet set. Before launch, the business must specify how long enquiries and completed service records are kept, when they are deleted, and how backup copies are handled.

5. Your choices

  • You can browse services without submitting a request.
  • Email and job details are optional.
  • You can remove demo requests by clearing this website's stored data in your browser settings. This removes the saved summaries from that browser.
  • Do not use a shared browser to submit personal details into this demo.

For the live service, contact the business to ask about information held about you, request corrections or deletion, or withdraw permission for future contact. The business should explain any records it needs to retain and why.
