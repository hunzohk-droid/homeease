FAQ

Questions people ask first

How do I book a service?

Choose a service, send a request with a few details, then confirm next steps with our team. You can start from the homepage search or the services list.

Do I pay on the website?

Pricing and payment details are shared after we review your request. This site is for finding help and sending the job details, not checkout.

Which areas do you cover?

We currently list placeholder neighborhoods around the metro. Share your location in the request form and we’ll confirm if we can help.

Can I request something that isn’t listed?

Yes. Pick the closest service, or use Contact to describe the task. We’ll let you know if it’s a fit.

How soon will someone get back to me?

Most requests are reviewed within one business day. Timing can vary with the type of job and the date you need.
