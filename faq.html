Frequently asked questions

Find practical answers about quotes, scheduling, and requesting help for your home.

Demo website: Service requests are saved only in your browser and are not sent to HomeEase. The answers below describe the planned service process.

1. How do I get a quote?

Choose a service and complete the request form with your location and job details. Include information such as the size of the area, the problem, or the items that need work.

On the live website, our team will review your request and discuss the scope and quote with you. If more information or an assessment is needed, this will be explained before work is agreed.

2. Can I choose my preferred date and time?

Yes. The request form lets you choose a preferred date and a Morning, Afternoon, or Evening time window. These are preferences and depend on availability.

Submitting a request does not reserve a slot. Our team will discuss an available appointment with you.

3. How do cancellations and rescheduling work?

Contact us as soon as you need to cancel or change an appointment. Include your request reference number so the team can identify your enquiry.

A new date depends on availability. Ask about the applicable notice period and any cancellation or rescheduling charges before agreeing to an appointment.

4. Who provides the materials and equipment?

This depends on the service and the agreed quote. Tell us whether you already have materials, replacement parts, paint, or equipment for the job.

Check the service's included work and exclusions. Our team will clarify what you need to supply and whether any additional materials are included in the price.

5. Which areas do you cover?

See the service areas on our Contact page and the area dropdown in the request form. The current demo contains sample areas rather than confirmed business coverage.

If your location is not listed, contact us to ask about coverage before making plans.

6. How is my service request confirmed?

After submitting the form, you receive a reference number and a summary of your request. This acknowledges the request; it does not confirm an appointment.

On the live website, our team will contact you to confirm availability and pricing. In this demo, no request is sent and no team member is notified.

7. How much will my service cost?

Pricing depends on the type of service, the amount of work, the condition of the area or item, and any materials required. Services currently display “Request a quote.”

The scope and final price should be agreed before work starts. If a starting price is displayed in the future, check what it includes and what could change the total.

8. Who can I contact for other questions?

Visit the Contact page for phone, email, WhatsApp, and opening hours. Include your service name or request reference when asking about an existing enquiry.

The current contact details are placeholders. Real business contact information will be added before launch.

Still have a question?

Visit our contact page for more information, or explore the services available for your home.
